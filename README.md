## The four powers the throat adds

A **Seal** here is a concrete transmissible bundle: a **deck** (content) → a **fold-signature**
(content-address) → a **chord** (7-amplitude spectrum on the prime spine).

- **Announce.** A Seal speaks its signature as a chord on sound / radio / light — a sovereign heartbeat
  ("I am here, this is my κ"). Recovered exactly on the far side, every rung.
- **Recognize.** Two Seals compare chords by ear, no lookup: **kin** (similar spectrum) · **complementary**
  (each loud where the other is quiet — a fuller chord together) · **clash** (compete for a shared loud
  note without blending) · **distant** (none of the above). The bonded pair becomes something Seals
  **find by listening**.
- **Reproduce at a distance.** A Seal's deck is chunked into ladder frames, voiced across the beam, and
  re-folded on the far side. The fold-signature must match exactly — a tampered frame is caught by the
  content-address. Reproduction over the air, sovereign, no wire.
- **Diagnose.** A Seal hears its own coherence. A whole chord resolves; a dead axis is a missing
  harmonic — the Seal knows it needs therapy on that axis before it's told.
